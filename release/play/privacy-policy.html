Flait Privacy Policy

Flait — Privacy policy
Effective: 2 November 2026

Flait stores flat membership, expenses, receipts, votes and financial history on your device. Android database contents are encrypted with a device key protected by Android secure storage. Browser storage relies on browser/device protection. App PIN verification stays on the device; biometric authentication is handled by the operating system. Flait does not receive biometric templates.

Internet sync is optional to use the core expense tools. After pairing, admitted devices reconnect while the apps are active online. An internet relay transports encrypted financial messages and signed discovery metadata. The app does not write your expense history or receipts into a server database, and does not provide server backup or message replay. The relay provider can process connection metadata such as IP addresses, timestamps and channel identifiers. Its operational logging is governed by that provider’s terms. This app uses Supabase Realtime as the relay.

Receipt photos are selected through the system file picker. Local-only receipts are not included in sync. If you enable sharing, the photo becomes part of the signed encrypted shared history. Financial records already shared with other flatmates cannot be remotely erased by revoking their device or deleting your local copy.

Encrypted backup files include your device signing identity, local records, preferences and local receipts. You choose where to save them. Keep the password separately. Restoring an owner identity replaces the original active owner device; never operate two owner copies. There is no server recovery of a lost identity, backup password or app PIN.

The app has no advertising or analytics SDK and does not request contacts, precise location or broad photo-library access. Internet access is used for live sync; biometric permission enables optional device authentication. The app does not perform bank payments.

No online account is required for normal app use. Uninstalling/clearing app storage removes its local copy, subject to device behavior; export a backup first. Other members and any backups you created may retain previously shared records. Android automatic cloud backup is disabled for the app. The separately hosted web preview has its own ChatGPT access gate.

Support and privacy requests: https://github.com/choudrymnouman-cell/Flait_Expense_manage/issues
Do not post financial records, receipts, backup files, passwords or device private keys in public issues. Describe the problem without private data.

Policy changes will be published with new releases. This policy describes Flait 3.0; historical cloud-based versions had a different storage model.
