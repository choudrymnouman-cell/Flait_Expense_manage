# Flait — Shared Expenses

Short description (under 80 characters):
Offline flat expenses, fair splits, member dues and encrypted device sync.

Full description:
Flait helps flatmates track shared expenses while keeping their financial history on their own devices.

• Record expenses offline, with automatic date and time.
• Split bills equally, by percentage or by custom amounts.
• Track member dues, shared pool contributions and reimbursements separately.
• Approve claims with signed member votes and preserve correction history.
• Find expenses by member, category, status or date; export PDF and CSV reports.
• Keep recurring monthly bill templates and submit each month after review.
• Attach a local receipt, or explicitly share it in encrypted flat history.
• Pair members once and reconnect automatically while apps are active online.
• Protect the Android local database with device-key encryption and optionally lock the app with a PIN or device authentication.
• Export password-encrypted backups and use the phone replacement guide.
• Use dark mode and an English/Urdu core interface; technical messages may remain in English.

Important: the current flat owner must be online to confirm queued work. Both apps must be active online for live exchange; Android background execution is not guaranteed. The relay transports encrypted messages without storing the app’s financial journal in a server database. Provider connection metadata may still be processed.

Flait records payments; it does not move bank funds. No online account or advertising is required. Keep backups: there is no server recovery for a lost identity or forgotten backup password. Use one active owner device.

Suggested category: Finance (budgeting / expense tracking; not banking or lending).
Support: GitHub repository issues; never post financial records or backups publicly.
Developer support email, legal identity/address, countries, pricing and target-audience declarations must be confirmed in the owner’s Play Console.
